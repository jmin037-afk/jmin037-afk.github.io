Privacy Policy

ODL Subcontractor Sync is an internal tool used only by One Design Lab, Inc. It is not offered to the public.

What it accesses: the One Design Lab company Gmail account, with read-only permission. It cannot send, delete or change email.

How data is used: sender names, email addresses, signatures and project details are used only to identify subcontractors and add their business contact information to One Design Lab's internal subcontractor list. Data is not sold or shared with third parties, except that email text is processed by Anthropic's Claude AI service to classify senders.

Storage: results are stored only in One Design Lab's own Google Sheet.
